# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Agents Kit to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas, not personal attacks.
- Welcome questions from newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and helping its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained
disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the
maintainer's public GitHub profile. Please include relevant links and context, and avoid
sharing sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. The maintainer may remove comments, restrict
participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation. The project is currently
maintained by an individual, so response times are best-effort.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue and pull request discussions, and other
spaces where someone is representing the Agents Kit project.
